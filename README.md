# Egg Thieves

An original game Max came up with for Tommy's scout campout,
loosely based on Flunkyball and Kubb. Two teams (1 or 2
players each), a gate of 3 wooden blocks in the middle of
the field, and a big basket of 24 eggs at the far end.
Break the gate with a flicked log, run and steal eggs one
at a time, and bring them home while the other team
rebuilds the gate and then chases you.

Play it here: https://maxjwallner.github.io/egg-thieves/

Play solo against the computer, or online with friends
using room codes. Optional twists in the settings by the
online buttons: basket stealing (raid the other basket,
guard zones included) and players per team (1 or 2).
